Why Simplified Calculation

In math, we can sometimes make a problem easier by not following the exact order the numbers first appear in.

When we understand the laws and properties of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division, we can rearrange the numbers to get the same answer in a simpler way. This is called simplified calculation.

Example:

Let’s take 25+37+75.

Notice that

25+75=10025 + 75 = 100

So, add those first:

25+75=100→100+37=13725 + 75 = 100 \longrightarrow 100 + 37 = 137

This is quicker and clearer than adding strictly from left to right.

Why This Works

  • We use the commutative property (change the order in addition or multiplication).

  • These rules keep the value the same while letting us choose an easier path.
