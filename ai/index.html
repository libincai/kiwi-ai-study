AI is part of your everyday world

You don't need to be a programmer to start understanding AI. Begin with things you already know.

📱

On your phone

Face unlock, photo enhancement, voice typing and video recommendations can all involve AI.

🎒

At school

AI can help explain a tricky science idea, brainstorm a story, or create extra practice questions — but you still need to think.

⚽

In your interests

From sports analysis and music suggestions to game characters and creative projects, AI can be a useful tool to explore.

A learning path, not a pile of technical words

We start with what AI is, then learn how to communicate with it, understand its building blocks, and finally explore how AI can work through tasks.
