AI Knowledge Classroom

AI Knowledge Classroom on Kiwi 爱AI Study offers short lessons for curious students, parents, and teachers. AI is bigger than one app or chatbot—we explain it through school life and everyday examples so the ideas stay clear and approachable.

You will meet core topics—models, prompts, context, memory, generative AI, and agents—with illustrations and bilingual pages you can read at your own pace. Episodes connect, but you may start with whatever question you have today.
