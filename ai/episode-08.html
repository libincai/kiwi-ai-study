What Is a Prompt?

A prompt is how you tell AI what you want. AI may be powerful, but it cannot read your mind.

AI cannot read your mind

Imagine a very fast, powerful horse. You climb on and say, “Run!” The horse can run — but where should it go? Without a destination, its power alone cannot take you where you want to be.

AI is similar. It can write, explain, create and help with complex tasks, but it does not know the details you have not provided. It needs to understand your goal, situation, requirements and the result you want.

A school-life example

Suppose you ask: “Write an absence email.” AI can produce an email, but it does not know who the email is for, why the student will be absent, how long the absence will be, or what tone you prefer.

Now try: “Write a short, polite email to my child's teacher. My child is sick and will be absent from school tomorrow for one day.” The extra details help AI understand the request more clearly.

Try this everyday example

Try it yourself: ask AI to explain a math problem. Include your grade, the topic you are studying, what part confuses you and whether you want a hint or a step-by-step explanation.

A prompt does not need to be complicated

The key is not to write the longest possible prompt. The key is to include the information that matters. A clear prompt gives AI a better direction and makes it easier for you to judge whether the answer meets your needs.
